Why custom terminal boxes are hard to get right

Standard sizes never quite fit.
Off-the-shelf certs don't always apply.

  • ✕Catalogue terminal boxes that are close but need modification on site — adding cost and compromising IP ratings
  • ✕Suppliers who quote quickly but can't provide ATEX certification documentation when the site audit arrives
  • ✕Overseas manufacturers with 8–12 week lead times and minimum order quantities that don't suit one-off or small-batch projects
  • ✕Vague delivery windows that push project timelines — and leave you explaining delays to a client
  • ✕Receiving a box that's right on paper but wrong in practice — wrong gland positions, wrong cable entry size, wrong mounting arrangement

How indEx solves this

We manufacture custom terminal boxes to your drawing or specification — not from a catalogue. Every dimension, cable entry position, gland thread, and IP rating is built to what you actually need, not what's nearest on a shelf.

Our engineering team reviews your spec before we quote. If we see a problem — a dimension that won't work, a material that's marginal for the environment — we tell you before a pound is spent on manufacture.

For hazardous area sites, our ATEX-certified terminal boxes are built and documented to Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 21 and Zone 22 requirements, with full certification packs ready for your site inspection.

All manufactured in Ashford, Kent. Standard lead time 15 working days from drawing approval.

What we build

Four material families.
All built to your spec.

Mild Steel

Steel Terminal Boxes

Powder-coated mild steel for industrial indoor and sheltered outdoor installations. Cost-effective, robust, fast to manufacture. Standard IP66 rating. RAL colour options available.

Most Specified

Stainless Steel

304 or 316 stainless for wash-down environments, coastal installations, food and pharmaceutical applications. Superior corrosion resistance where mild steel isn't suitable. IP66/67/68 options.

GRP / Fibreglass

GRP Terminal Boxes

Glass reinforced polyester for chemical, water treatment, and corrosive environments. Lighter than steel, excellent UV and chemical resistance. Ideal for outdoor and coastal plant.

ATEX Certified

ATEX Terminal Boxes

Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 21 & Zone 22 certified terminal boxes with full IECEx / ATEX documentation. Built for hazardous area installations in oil & gas, chemical, water treatment, and pharmaceutical sites.
